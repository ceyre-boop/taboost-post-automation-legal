Post one video everywhere.

Add a clip once and Post Everywhere puts it on every account you choose. Setup takes about two minutes.

You sign in yourself.

Each platform opens in the app's own Chrome window and you log in there. The app never asks for, sees or stores a password.

It posts the way you would.

It uses each site's normal upload page, with a pause between posts so your accounts stay in good standing.

Everything stays on this Mac.

Your clips, your sign-ins and your history live in your own app folder. Nothing is sent to us.

Connect your accounts.

Click Connect and sign in the way you normally do, in the Chrome window that opens. Chrome can fill in your saved passwords. Then come back here and press Done. You only do this once per account: it stays signed in.

Connect at least one account to continue.

You're all set.

Try a preview first.

Preview fills in everything on each site and stops right before the Post button, so you can see it work without anything going public.

With spacing off, several posts can hit the same account seconds apart. That can get an account flagged.
