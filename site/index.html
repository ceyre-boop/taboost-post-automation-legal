How it works

Three steps. About five minutes.

Posting the same video in five apps means uploading it five times and typing the caption five times. Do it once here.

1

Install it

Download the app and paste in your key. There is nothing else to set up.

2

Sign in to your accounts

A browser window opens. Sign in to each platform the way you always do. You only do this once.

3

Add a video and press Post

Pick the video, write the caption, tick the platforms. It uploads to each one while you do something else.
