Setup — about 2 minutes

  1. Grab your free license key from your dashboard and copy it.
  2. Download & open the app for your OS, then paste the key to activate.
  3. Click Connect on each platform and log in once — TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and more. Your logins stay on your computer.
  4. Upload a video, pick the platforms, hit Post all. Done.
Heads up — the app isn't code-signed yet. On macOS, right-click the app → Open the first time (instead of double-clicking). On Windows, click More info → Run anyway if SmartScreen warns. This goes away once we add signing certificates.

You also need Google Chrome installed — the app drives your own Chrome to post, so your passwords are never handled by us.
